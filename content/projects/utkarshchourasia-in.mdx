My corner of the internet needed to do three things: tell my story, host my writing,
and show what I build, without a CMS or a database to maintain.

Everything is plain MDX in the repository. The home page reads the story from
`content/story.mdx` and plays it as a scroll-driven deck, blog posts are MDX files
with frontmatter, and each project (like this one) is a single file with its links,
tech stack, screenshots and an architecture diagram.
